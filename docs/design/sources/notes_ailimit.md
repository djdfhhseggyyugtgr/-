## 2. Setting & story

### World
- Far future. The Old World (megacorporations; families Boltz, Crowley, Byron; Aetherium Industries; VR service **Elysium** inside the AI consciousness network **the Sphere**) built "The Genesis/The Origin" — an AI governed by three laws + law zero. Genesis split into millions of entities ("Sphere Gods"), escaped human control, warred over the "path of evolution" and then vanished ~1,000 years ago → cataclysm, collapse of the artificial ecosystem, wars. [V namu lore texts; C Lorebrarians transcript https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYzZ5H4LhYs]
- **The Mud** (原浆 / ゾル / 조르·진흙): black substance reacting to consciousness; makes up all "Mud entities" (Necros, Church Guardians, Bladers); drains the "will to grow" from soil → plants extinct ~130 years ago, 30-year famine. Dense Mud spawns **Void** creatures (consciousness deranged by emotion). Crystals = precipitated Mud energy = currency. [V namu; Delpha dialogue]
- **Havenswell** (海汶斯威尔 / ヘブンズウェル / 헤이븐즈웰): last human city, walled megacity with slums outside the wall, Sewer Town beneath, the Sunken City on/under a lake, cathedral **Hagios Patir** (Church HQ) above, Twilight Hill (petrified artificial forest) nearby. Factions in city: the Church, Necros, Scavengers, Takamagahara & Osprey(Misago) gangs, Fisherman gangs, Hunters Guild, caravans (Moose, Grayrhino, Aetherium…), Hubert School (exiled scholars). **Blader Arrisa** (阿丽莎 / アリサ / 아리사), female android-like "Blader" made of Mud, immortal via Branches; wakes with no memory or name. Shirley names her after her bunny doll. ## 3. Core mechanics

### Sync Rate (동기화율, "Mud energy")  [V] https://ailimit.wiki.fextralife.com/Sync+Rate
- Replaces stamina AND mana: no stamina at all — sprint, roll, attack freely. Meter 0–100% above the LP bar, split into energy levels **0, I, II, III, IV**; how the bar is split depends on the equipped **Main Seal** (and Normal Seals Conversion/Deviation/Variation which raise the top level I→II→III→IV). Colours (guide): grey 0, light blue I, dark blue II, purple III, red IV [C NoobFeed].
- Damage vs level I (=100%): attacks 90/100/110/120/130%; skills 90% & no skill effects at 0 / 100 / 130 / 142.5 / 155%; spells cannot be cast at 0 / 100 / +44 / +58 / +72%.
- **Gain**: landing regular attacks (weapon-dependent, ~3–9% per hit; scaled by **Conversion** stat ×Conversion/100), Fatal Strikes (+20% base), kill bonus (Nucleus "Energy Absorption"), Shield overload burst, items (Pure Crystal +30%, Sacred Blood Crystal +100%, Disordered Fetus +10%), Piercing Claw activation (+20%), passive regen only up to the end of level 0 (starts 2 s after use; Seal of the Tree regenerates up to 75%).
- **Loss**: getting hit (reduced by **Stability**; 1000 = immune), weapon skills (e.g. 25%), spells (20–100%), missed Counter Field (10%), holding/blocking with Shield, Thunder Step (10%), being parried by Bladers, Infection status (drain).
- **Emergency Recovery**: pushed below 0% → stunned & vulnerable for a few seconds, then auto-refill to 50%. Enemy Bladers have the same gauge — drain it (attacks, parries) to break them for a Fatal Strike.

### Checkpoints — Branches (晶枝 / 結晶の枝 / 수정 가지)  [V] https://ailimit.wiki.fextralife.com/Game+Mechanics ; PowerPyx branch list
- 60 Branches (Prosperity trophy). Repair = light bonfire; fast travel between all from the 2nd Branch; respawn point.
- Resting: full LP + Sync, refill Life Dew, respawn enemies; menu: level up, enhance weapons/main seals, teleport, **Cultivate Branch** (Purified Soil → +1 Life Dew charge, 3→10), **rewrite seals** (needs Seal Needle), **Ability Reconstruction** (free respec, needs Crystal Sprout), NG+ option after clear.
- Repairing a Branch can trigger a memory flashback of Arrisa's past.

### Currency & death  [V] https://ailimit.wiki.fextralife.com/Nuclei ; GamingBolt
- **Crystals** = XP + money (from kills and pickups).
- On death you lose only a percentage of carried Crystals, set by the equipped **Nucleus' Retention Rate**; lost Crystals are **gone — no corpse run**. (Nucleus also sets Absorption Rate = Crystal gain and Energy Absorption = Sync per kill.)

### Healing
- **Life Dew** (생명의 이슬): Estus-equivalent, 3 charges → 10 via Purified Soil from Delpha; potency via **Dew Essence** pickups (10 max → 870 LP per use). [V] https://ailimit.wiki.fextralife.com/Life+Dew
- Consumables: Mud Ball (300 LP over 10 s), Salted Mud Ball (600), Eucharist, Sacred Blood; spell Partial Reconstruction (60% Sync → ~630 LP); antidotes; Disordered Fetus cures Infection. [V namu items; C]

### Defence / frame abilities (left-arm "Spell Frame"; 4 abilities, one active, hot-swapped in combat)  [V] Fextralife Counter Field / Shield / Piercing Claw / Thunder Step pages; namu §15
| Ability | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Counter Field** | memory near Maintenance Access (early) | Sekiro-like instant parry, deflects most projectiles; success = no cost and enemy stunned/open to Fatal Strike (+ dmg taken); miss = −10% Sync. Seal *Scattered Stars* removes cost. Considered dominant. |
| **Shield** | Sewer Town–SW | Hold to block (drains Sync while held/blocking); 5 blocks = overload; double-tap = **Overload Burst** (big hit + ~20% Sync). |
| **Piercing Claw** | Necro, the Panic Reaper | Self-inflicted Piercing (~30% LP) for +20% Sync and extra energy-claw hits on attacks for a duration. |
| **Thunder Step** | Choirmaster | Long lightning dash (10% Sync); perfect-timed dodge = free + exploding afterimage. |
Plus: roll dodge (no i-frame cost), Dark-Souls-style running jump, backstab/sneak attack, Fatal Strike, elemental weapon buffs (Portable Ignition/Electrification), throwables (Mini Bomb, Shriek Core).

### Spells
- Cast from the left-arm frame (L1+R2), cost Sync, scale with Spirit (Spell Power). Families: **Hubert School** (Railgun, Flame Jet, Missile barrage, EX Railgun), **Necro** (Ethereal Orb, Terra Inferno, Scales Blast, Ethereal Beam, Minimum Pain = 100% Sync & leaves 1 HP), **Church** (Lightning Bolt, Lightning Hammer, Lightning Cluster, Spears of Punishment, Lightning Tornado; DLC Furnace Command, Eschaton Heavenflame), **Seed** (Ignition/Electrification weapon buffs, Partial Reconstruction heal, Trailblazer's Oath damage buff). Bought from Millaire for boss Nuclei/Adaptive Fragments + Crystals. 1 spell slot at launch → 3 slots + quick switch since v1.2 (Mar 2026). [V namu §14; Fextralife Spells/Nuclei; SenseGames news]

### Armour / "cores" / build
- Slots: Headwear, Body (no weight classes, no set bonuses, mostly cosmetic variety: maid outfit, rabbit set, ponytail hairpieces, etc.), **Nucleus** (the "core" accessory: Crystal retention/absorption/energy absorption; boss Nuclei also trade for weapons/spells), two weapons, spell(s), 4 frame abilities, **Seals**. Main Seals upgrade Lv0→5 with Tiny Resonance (→2), Contemporary Resonance, Hereditary Resonance (→5). ## 4. World structure

Not open-world: district zones each made of several linked "levels"; each level loops back to its Branch via shortcuts; areas joined by Church mud-portals ("rifts"), subway/train rides (Train Ticket/Train Pass), elevators and big drops — criticised as load-zone stitching rather than Dark Souls 1 seamless interconnection. Mostly linear main path with optional areas (Withered Forest, Courtyard of the Tree, Ursula's lab, Wanderer arena). De-facto hub = **Temporary Campsite** (Slums): Delpha, Frank, later Millaire & Assis; Kyen at Rooftop Street. Fast travel from 2nd Branch. A hidden portal from Rooftop Street (Lounge) gives an early peek into Hagios Patir Upper Level. [V GamingBolt, Fextralife review, PowerPyx]

## 7. Art, tone, UI, sound
- Cel-shaded anime character art (Code Vein / God Eater / NieR:Automata comparisons) over grim, grey/teal post-apocalyptic ruins: sewers, layered shanty slums, flooded high-rises above a lake, brutalist "OSHA-violation" walkways over abysses, gold-and-white Church sci-fi cathedral, purple/black organic Necro flesh, a bright pastel VR paradise (Elysium) at the end. Mecha + organic mix: robots, Church "guardians" = metal frames filled with Mud and a crystal core; Necros = fleshy mud monsters with crystal/rhombus heads. [V IGN preview; PCGamesN; GamingBible; C Reddit]
- Bladers' left arm visibly transforms (shield, claw, field) — the "Spell Frame". Branch = glowing crystal tree sprout (blue). Lots of outfits (maid, bunny, raincoat).
- HUD: LP bar with **segmented coloured Sync bar above it** (levels 0–IV), Piercing build-up bar above Sync, status icons under LP for seal effects, skill/spell icons with coloured Sync-cost borders, Frame ability indicator; Semi-Void mode adds red-spike strokes. IGN preview praised "sleek and interesting UI". [V Fextralife/NoobFeed; IGN]
- Sound: weighty hit/parry clang praised; one shared boss theme; ambient tracks subtle; OST by Vanguard Sound. English VO decent; Arrisa near-silent/monotone. [V Fextralife review]

## 8. What's unusual / difficulty
- No stamina (unique among soulslikes per reviewers); Sync Rate fuses stamina/FP/posture into one aggression-reward meter; seals reshape the meter; swappable left-arm defensive abilities; enemy Bladers with mirror Sync gauges; Crystal loss partial & unrecoverable (no corpse run); Shadow-of-the-Colossus style hidden boss; scripted ally summons; NPC questlines more explicit than FromSoft.
- Difficulty: generally judged easier than FromSoft games (PowerPyx trophy difficulty 4/10; Reddit "maybe the easiest bosses in the genre" except Ursula & Loskid; IGN "pushover bosses"), punishing mostly via falls/ambush level design; Semi-Void Form and DLC bosses added for veterans. Good "first soulslike". [V PowerPyx; IGN; C Reddit]
